# Raw reports of the literature sweep (2026-11-24)

These are the final messages of the five reader subagents of the sweep, saved as they were
returned, plus the Hexagon reader's table of relevant items. They are working reports, not
refereed text. `lit/SURVEY.md` sections 5 and 6 are the checked account; where the two differ,
the survey is the corrected one.

- `hexagon.md`, `hexagon_relevant_items.md`: the Hexagon preprint server.
- `github.md`: issue threads and 27 cloned repositories, including the search for anyone else's
  transfer theorem.
- `crossing.md`: a digest of the notes on crossing decompositions in
  `1337isnot1337/integer-factoring`. Every claim in it is that repository's own.
- `arxiv.md`: arXiv, ECCC (blocked after TR26-265) and Zenodo.
- `palomar_experts.md`: the Palomar registry and expert commentary.

The readers' downloads, clones and scripts are on the data disk under `/mnt/data/review/sweep/`
and are not in this repository.
